Engagements

Six projects that shaped the practice

AI search platform, DoD Office of Inspector General

Designed and led a retrieval-augmented generation platform that lets some 2,000 people in more than 60 locations search years of reports and guidance in plain language. It changed how the office finds what it already knows.

Oracle ERP migration, U.S. Treasury

Led the migration of seven legacy financial systems onto Oracle Financials, in one of the most complex financial environments in the federal government. The result was financial reporting in real time and books that stand up to audit.

IT governance framework, DoD Office of Inspector General

Built the framework that ties DoD policy, the Enterprise Architecture Review Board and portfolio management together, so that leadership could see and steer a technology portfolio spread across many sites.

Financial systems modernization, FEMA (with IBM)

Deputy project manager on the program that replaced FEMA's core financial system through shared services and outsourcing. Owned the project plans, schedule and action items, the work order management plans, the financial systems strategy and the business cases that went to leadership.

Financial management systems consolidation, Department of the Navy

Led the effort to bring several financial management systems into one enterprise solution: project plans and charters, SharePoint requirements, status reporting, acceptance testing, standard templates and the stakeholder work that makes a consolidation stick. With Booz Allen Hamilton.

Cloud migration, DoD and Office of Naval Research

As part of a Booz Allen Hamilton team, led the cloud migration and application rationalization for the Department of Defense and the Office of Naval Research: retiring legacy applications and modernizing the infrastructure under mission-critical systems.
