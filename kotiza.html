Software

Kotiza, the digital tontine registry.

A tontine is a rotating savings group. Kotiza keeps its register for the organizer, in Senegal and across the diaspora: who has paid, who is due, whose turn it is. Reminders go out over WhatsApp and SMS under the Kotiza name. Kotiza keeps the record; it never holds anyone's money.

Visit getkotiza.com

Messaging

What Kotiza sends, and what it doesn't

Kotiza sends its members service messages only: one-time sign-in codes, contribution reminders and payout notices. They go out over WhatsApp, through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform, and by SMS. Kotiza never uses them for advertising. A member who wants to stop receiving them can ask the organizer of their tontine, or write to the address below.

Questions about Kotiza, how it is run or its messaging: info@ascendintelligences.com.
