Software

Kotiza, the digital tontine registry.

Kotiza keeps the register of tontines — rotating savings groups — for organizers in Senegal and across the diaspora: contributions, schedules and payout order, with reminders sent over WhatsApp and SMS under the Kotiza name. Kotiza never holds funds; it is a register, not a wallet.

Visit getkotiza.com

Ownership & operation

Two companies, one owner

Kotiza is a product of the Ascend Intelligences group. Both companies below are owned and managed by the same principal, Madogal Tall, and the Kotiza name is used by each of them for its own part of the service.

Ascend Intelligences LLC

Limited liability company registered in the State of Maryland, United States (SDAT Department ID W27302025). Publishes this website and holds Kotiza's messaging infrastructure: the WhatsApp Business account and the SMS sender identities used to reach members under the Kotiza name.

Ascend International Professional Service SUARL

Single-member limited liability company registered in Dakar, Senegal (RCCM SN.DKR.2024.B.30459, NINEA 011403467). Operates Kotiza in Senegal: publisher of the service at getkotiza.com, party to its terms of use and privacy policy, and billing entity for organizers.

Messaging

Messages sent under the Kotiza name

Kotiza sends its members transactional messages only: one-time sign-in codes, contribution reminders and payout notices. They are sent over WhatsApp (Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform) and by SMS, and are never used for advertising. Members can ask to stop receiving them at any time through the organizer of their tontine or by writing to the address below.

Questions about Kotiza, its operation or its messaging: info@ascendintelligences.com.
