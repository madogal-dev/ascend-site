Advice on financial management, IT governance and digital transformation for public institutions, development organizations and private companies.

Ascend Intelligences works with U.S. federal agencies, international development organizations and private companies. The work is practical: modernizing financial systems, putting real governance around technology, running the projects that deliver both, and bringing artificial intelligence, including AI agents, into finance and audit offices in a way that holds up when the auditors arrive.

Behind the firm are fifteen years spent inside demanding public institutions, among them the U.S. Treasury, the Department of Defense Office of Inspector General and the Department of Justice, and two certifications that count in this field: PMP in project management and CISA in information systems audit.

Software

Kotiza, the digital tontine registry

Kotiza is the software product of Ascend Intelligences LLC. In Senegal it is run by the firm's sister company, Ascend International Professional Service SUARL in Dakar. It keeps the register of a tontine for its organizer, in Senegal and in the diaspora. Kotiza keeps the record; it never holds the money.
