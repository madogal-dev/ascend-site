PMP

Project & Program Management

Running the project is often the part that decides whether a modernization lands. I have managed or co-managed financial system programs from the inside: deputy project manager on FEMA's financial systems modernization with IBM, the consolidation of the Department of the Navy's financial management systems with Booz Allen Hamilton, the Oracle migration at the Treasury, and project support for the MIL Corporation's federal clients.   • PMO set-up, performance indicators and status reporting to senior leadership
  • Project appraisal and scoring models for investment committees and boards
