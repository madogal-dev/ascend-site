Privacy

What this site does with your information.

This notice covers ascendintelligences.com and the forms on it. Last updated in October 2026.

Who is responsible
Ascend Intelligences LLC, North Potomac, Maryland, United States, publishes this site. Orders for the Senegal toolkit are handled by Ascend International Professional Service SUARL, Dakar. For any question about your information: info@ascendintelligences.com.
What is collected
Only what you type in a form: your name, e-mail address, organization, the subject you choose and your message. The download and order forms also record which page the request came from.
Why
To reply to you, send the files you asked for, or handle your order. Your details are not sold, not shared with advertisers and not added to a mailing list.
Who receives it
Your message reaches our mailbox through one of two form services based in the United States: Formspree for the contact form, FormSubmit for the download and order forms. The mailbox is hosted by Google Workspace.
How long it is kept
As long as needed to answer you and follow up. Messages are deleted after 24 months unless we are working together.
Your rights
You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by writing to info@ascendintelligences.com. You will get an answer within 30 days.
Cookies and tracking
This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising tracker. Your browser keeps your language choice on your own device; it is never sent to us. The fonts come from Google Fonts, which, like any web server, sees the address of the device that requests them.
