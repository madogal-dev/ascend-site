From the checklist

Six of the thirty-two points

Enterprise risk management is no longer a required framework. It only appears under “Other Considerations and Best Practices” (Section VII).

Every documented risk gets a tag: fraud, improper payments, information security, significant change, or a combination (Section IV.A).

Deficiencies now go to the Inspector General as well as up the line (Section IV.C).

If a relevant principle is not achieved, that is a material weakness (Table 2). The seventeen principles have become the severity test.

Each deficiency needs a root-cause analysis, done by management. The circular says plainly that this is not the auditors' job (Section IV.E).

There is a single annual report with a fixed label, “Analysis of Entity's Systems, Controls and Legal Compliance,” and it should carry only essential information (Section V).
