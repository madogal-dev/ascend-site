A preview of the checklist

Six of the thirty-two points

Enterprise risk management is no longer a required framework. It appears only under “Other Considerations and Best Practices” (Section VII).

Each documented risk is tagged as fraud, improper payments, information security, significant change, or a combination (Section IV.A).

Deficiencies go to the next supervisory level and to the Inspector General (Section IV.C).

Non-achievement of a relevant principle is a material weakness (Table 2). The seventeen principles are now the severity test.

A root-cause analysis exists for each deficiency — management's job, not the auditors' (Section IV.E).

One report, one label: “Analysis of Entity's Systems, Controls and Legal Compliance,” with only essential information (Section V).
